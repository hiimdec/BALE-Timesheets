The night before

The call sheet arrives by email, as ever, at about eleven at night. Share it into TimeMachine and it reads the invoicing details straight off the sheet, then shows you what it found so you can check every field before anything is saved. The job is set up before the kettle has boiled.

02

On set

Set your call time in the app and the day starts running. After that the lock screen does the work: Lunch, and Wrap. Both are two-tap, arm then confirm, because a phone in your back pocket should not be able to wrap your day.

If lunch gets cut short, Back early? records the curtailment. That is time you worked that would otherwise quietly go missing: recorded, and paid.

Getting production to pay on time?

That’s outside my scope.

If they don’t, TimeMachine handles that bit too. It flags the invoice overdue, and it will remind you on the due date if you want it to. When you decide to charge for it, it works out the statutory charges where applicable: interest at 8% over the Bank of England base rate, plus the fixed recovery fee. They go onto the invoice as their own section, and it drafts the chase email for you to send.

It cannot make anyone pay. It can make the asking quick, correct, and hard to argue with.
