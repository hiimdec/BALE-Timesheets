What it does

  • ▸ Calculates the day. Your role, your rates, OT and penalties worked out as the day runs.
  • ▸ Makes the timesheet. A clean A4 PDF for production. No more biro on the back of a call sheet.
  • ▸ Sends the invoice. One tap from timesheet to invoice. VAT-aware, auto-numbered, your bank details already on it.
On iPhone, it goes further
  • Call-sheet reader. Import a call sheet and it fills in your invoicing details for you, on-device. Nothing leaves the phone. iOS 26, Apple Intelligence
  • Live Activity. Your shoot day on the lock screen: running total, a live timer, two-tap Lunch / Wrap. iPhone
  • Siri. “Hey Siri, wrap now in TimeMachine” stamps the time, hands full of cable. iPhone
  • iCloud backup. A quiet daily snapshot in your own iCloud - nobody else can see it. New phone or reinstalled app, your shoots come back. iPhone
