## Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

### Day types — basic structure

| Day type | Length | OT starts after | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Working Day (Shoot) | 10 working hrs + 1 hr lunch (11h total) | 11h from call | BHR = BDR ÷ 10 |
| Continuous Working Day (CWD) | 9 hrs continuous | 9h from call | Triggered if 1st break missed (>6.5h) |
| Pre-light Day | 8 hrs + 1 hr lunch | 9h from call | Lunch provided / £7.50 if not |
| Prep / Recce / Build / Strike | 8 hrs (no lunch) | 8h from call | Lunch at producer's discretion |
| Rest Day | flat fee = BDR | n/a | No OT, no penalties |
| Travel Day (non-shoot) | min 5 hrs at BHR | n/a | Single time always |
| Night Shoot (call 17:00–05:00) | min 10 hrs | n/a | 2 × BHR flat, no OT, no triple time |

### Call-time bands (weekdays)

| Call time | Band | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Before 05:00 | Night Call | Night-shoot rules — 2 × BHR for all hrs, min 10h |
| 05:00–07:00 | Early Call | OT rate paid 05:00–07:00, then BDR from call |
| 07:00–11:00 | Standard Call | Normal — BDR for 11 hrs, OT after |
| 11:00–17:00 | Late Call | Day calculated **from 11:00**, OT after 11h from 11:00 |
| 17:00–05:00 | Night Call | Night-shoot rules — 2 × BHR for all hrs, min 10h |

### Overtime grades (Mon–Fri)

| Grade | BDR range | Coefficient |
|---|---|---|
| Grade I | £0 – £444 | 1.5 × BHR |
| Grade II | £445 – £676 | 1.25 × BHR |
| Grade III | £677+ | 1.0 × BHR |
| After-midnight (00:00–05:00) | any | 3 × BHR (triple time), continues to wrap if past 05:00 |

OT is rounded **up to 30 mins** (so 10 OT mins = 30 mins charged).

### Weekend / Bank Holiday multipliers

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday | 1.5 × BDR, min 10h, OT 1.5×BHR | 2 × BHR flat, min 10h | 1.5 × BDR, OT after 9h at 1.5×BHR | 1.5 × BHR for 8h, OT after 8h at 1.5×BHR | 1.5 × BHR for 8h, OT after 9h at 1.5×BHR | flat = BDR | min 5h × BHR |

### Breaks — penalties at a glance

| Break | Window | If late | If curtailed | If missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Break (1 hr, unpaid) | within 5.5h of call | £10 penalty | OT starts earlier by curtailment minutes; if no OT, paid that time at single | If not given by 6.5h: day becomes **CWD**, no late-lunch penalty. Otherwise: free meal **or** £7.50 allowance. On night shoot: 30m × BHR |
| 2nd Break (30 min, paid) | within 5.5h of end of 1st | Cannot be delayed — treated as missed | Pay for the curtailed minutes | 30m × BHR |
| CWD Break 1 (30 min, paid) | by 9h after call | n/a (can't be delayed) | n/a | 30m × BHR |
| CWD Break 2 (30 min, paid) | by 12.5h after call | n/a (can't be delayed) | n/a | 30m × BHR |

### Other key numbers

- **Mileage:** 50p/mile, return from W1F 9SE, only outside the M25 (or 20-mile radius for non-London prod cos).
- **Travel time:** single time always; first hour each way deducted; no payment unless travel + work ≥ 11h.
- **Air travel:** flight > 4h → no shoot that day. Flight > 8h → no shoot for 24h after arrival.
- **Time Off The Clock (TOC):** min 11h between wrap and next call (10h hard floor). One TOC hour worked = paid at standard OT rate **on top of** that day's BDR.
- **Holiday pay:** 12.07% of hours worked; included in payments unless agreed separately.
- **Invoice payment:** within 7 days of receipt for direct-contract crew.
- **Best practice:** don't schedule shoot days beyond 12h from main unit call (10 + 1 lunch + 1 OT).

> **Note:** Nearly every clause in §2–§6 explicitly excludes PMs, PAs and Runners — see Appendix 1 §(a) for their separate rules.

## 1 — Your Services

You provide the services of your grade for an agreed sum that is not less than the minimum and not more than the maximum for that grade in Appendix 1 (your "basic daily rate") on weekdays, plus any additional payments under this agreement.

Direct-contract crew invoices are paid within **7 days** of receipt. Services must be of a good professional standard consistent with what's reasonably expected of someone engaged at your grade.

All information obtained during engagement is **confidential**. Copyright in anything created during engagement is **assigned to the production company by way of future assignment**, and you waive all moral rights in respect of such work.

## 2 — Day Types and Best Practice

**Best practice:** APA members should not schedule shoot days beyond **12 hours** from main unit call (10 + 1 lunch + 1 OT) other than in exceptional circumstances. Where exceptional circumstances apply (e.g. talent / location availability), crew must be told the scheduled day length at the time of engagement so they can choose whether to accept. If OT beyond 12h becomes necessary, crew must be informed as soon as possible.

**Unit Call vs Department Call.** Unit Call is when the day officially starts. A Department Call is when the whole department starts (not an individual — e.g. a genny driver who collects equipment earlier). Throughout the document, "call time" = unit call time. Main unit call dictates the day type (continuous, night, etc.).

> §2 does not apply to PMs, PAs, and Runners — see Appendix 1.

### 2.1 — The Basic Working Day

11 hours total (10 working + 1 hr lunch). Paid at your **Basic Daily Rate (BDR)** per Appendix 1. **BHR = BDR ÷ 10.**

#### 2.1.1 — Standard Call (07:00–11:00, Mon–Fri)
- Day starts at call. 10 + 1 = 11h. OT after 11h.
- *Example:* Call 08:00, Wrap 22:00 → 14h day.

#### 2.1.2 — Night Call: call before 05:00 (Mon–Fri)
- Treated as night shoot. **2 × BHR** for every hour worked, minimum 10h call.
- If night-shoot call is on Sat/Sun, Mon–Fri night-shoot rule applies throughout the engagement (no "double-double").
- *Example:* Call 02:00, Wrap 15:00 → 13h (incl 1 unpaid lunch hour).

#### 2.1.3 — Early Call: 05:00–07:00 (Mon–Fri, applies all 7 days)
- Day starts at call. Hours **05:00–07:00 paid at OT rate**; rest of day = BDR. OT after 11h from call.
- *Example:* Call 05:00, Wrap 19:00 → 14h day; 05:00–07:00 paid at OT, 07:00–05:00 (yes, the 14h block from call) at BDR.
- **Pre-call work before 05:00** (e.g. genny driver): paid **3 × BHR (triple time)** until 05:00, then OT rate from 05:00 until department call.

#### 2.1.4 — Late Call: 11:00–17:00 (Mon–Fri)
- No matter when call is, **the basic working day is calculated from 11:00**. Still 11h total. OT after 11h from 11:00.
- *Example:* Call 13:00, Wrap 23:00 → 12h, counting from 11:00.

#### 2.1.5 — Night Call: 17:00–05:00 (Mon–Fri)
- All work commencing 17:00–05:00 = night shoot. **2 × BHR** for all hours, **min 10h call**.
- Breaks: same as standard day shoot (1st + 2nd break apply).
- **No OT rate, no triple-time-after-midnight** — flat 2 × BHR throughout, call to wrap.
- Going past 05:00 next day **does not** start a new engagement; you stay on 2 × BHR until wrap.
- For Sat 17:00+ or Sun 17:00+ night-shoot calls: 2 × BHR throughout, no "double-double".

### 2.2 — Continuous Working Day

Any day on which you work 9 hours **with no breaks**. Standard call window 07:00–11:00. Food and beverages provided at an appropriate time during the day. **OT applies after 9h** at the OT rate per Appendix 1. Saturday CWD: OT at 1.5 × BDR. Sunday CWD: OT at 2 × BDR.

**CWD breaks (paid, part of working day):**
- 30 min break after **9h** from call. If missed: 30m × BHR penalty.
- A further 30 min break after **12.5h** from call. If missed: 30m × BHR penalty.
- These breaks **cannot be delayed** — given on time or missed (penalty applies).

#### 2.2.1 — Standard Call CWD (07:00–11:00, Mon–Fri)
- 9h day from call; OT after 9h.
- *Example:* Call 08:00, Wrap 18:00.

#### 2.2.2 — CWD with Night Call before 05:00 (Mon–Fri)
- **Double basic daily rate (2 × BDR).** 9h day, OT after 9h at **2 × BHR**.
- *Example:* Call 03:00, Wrap 13:00.

#### 2.2.3 — CWD with Early Call 05:00–07:00 (Mon–Fri)
- Day starts at call. 05:00–07:00 paid at OT rate. Rest = 9h CWD from call. OT after 9h.
- Pre-05:00 work (e.g. genny driver): triple time until 05:00, then OT rate to dept call.
- *Example:* Call 05:00, Wrap 17:00.

#### 2.2.4 — CWD with Late Call 11:00–17:00 (Mon–Fri)
- Day calculated **from 11:00**. 9h total. OT after 9h from call.
- *Example:* Call 14:00, Wrap 22:00 (calculated from 11:00).

#### 2.2.5 — CWD with Night Call 17:00–05:00 (Mon–Fri)
- **2 × BDR.** 9h day. OT after 9h at **2 × BHR**.

### 2.3 — Non-Shooting Days

A working day on which shooting does not take place. Default: 8h, charged at BHR; OT after 8h at standard OT rate.

| Type | Day length | OT starts after | Lunch | Meal compensation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rest Day** | flat fee = BDR | n/a (no OT) | n/a | n/a (any day of the week) |
| **Prep Day** | 8h | 8h (9h if 1st break given) | producer's discretion | none |
| **Recce Day** | 8h | 8h (9h if 1st break given) | producer's discretion | none |
| **Build Day / Strike Day** | 8h | 8h (9h if 1st break given) | producer's discretion | none |
| **Pre-light Day** | 8h + 1h lunch | 9h | 1 hour break | £7.50 if meal not provided |

**Rest Day** = at production's request you remain on location but no filming. Flat BDR fee, no OT/penalties/meal compensation — applies any day of the week.

**Important exception:** §2.3 does **not** apply to **DOPs, Art Directors, and Location Managers** — for them every engagement day is a Basic Working Day (10+1, OT after 11h).

No food / food expenses obligation on non-shooting days **except** pre-light.

*Pre-light example:* Call 10:00, Wrap 21:00 → 8h + 1h lunch + 2h OT.

### 2.4 — Working on Saturdays, Sundays, Bank Holidays and Statutory Holidays

(i) **Basic Working Day on Saturday** — 1.5 × BDR for all hours worked, min 10h call.
(ii) **Basic Working Day on Sun/Bank Hol/Stat Hol** — 2 × BHR for all hours worked, min 10h call.
(iii) **Night Shoot on Saturday** — 2 × BHR for all hours, min 10h call.
(iv) **Night Shoot on Sun/Bank Hol** — 2 × BHR for all hours, min 10h call (no "double-double").
(v) **CWD on Saturday** — 1.5 × BDR. OT after 9h at 1.5 × BHR.
(vi) **CWD on Sun/Bank Hol** — 2 × BDR. OT after 9h at 2 × BHR.
(vii) **Prep / Recce / Build / Strike on Saturday** — 1.5 × BHR for 8h. OT after 8h at 1.5 × BHR.
(viii) **Prep / Recce / Build / Strike on Sun/Bank Hol** — 2 × BHR for 8h. OT after 8h at 2 × BHR.
(ix) **Pre-light on Saturday** — 1.5 × BHR for 8h. OT after 9h (lunch included) at 1.5 × BHR.
(x) **Pre-light on Sun/Bank Hol** — 2 × BHR for 8h. OT after 9h at 2 × BHR.
(xi) **Rest Day on Saturday** — flat BDR, no OT.
(xii) **Rest Day on Sun/Bank Hol** — flat BDR, no OT.
(xiii) **Travel Day on Saturday** — BHR regardless of time/day, min 5h call.
(xiv) **Travel Day on Sun/Bank Hol** — BHR regardless of time/day, min 5h call.

> Reminder: §2 does not apply to PMs, PAs, Runners — see Appendix 1 §(a).

## 3 — Travel

§3 does not apply to PMs, PAs or Runners.

### 3.1 — Travel Time

Always paid at **single time** (BHR), regardless of time or day of the week. **If travel time + working time totals less than 11 hours, no travel time is payable.**

| Scenario | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Travel on a non-shooting day** | Paid at BHR for hours travelled, **min 5 hours**. After a travel non-shoot day, the next working day is calculated **base to base** (e.g. hotel to hotel) — applies to all location shoots where crew stays in a hotel. |
| **Travel on a basic working day** | Starting point = **W1F 9SE** for London-based prod cos. Paid at BHR for time spent travelling **less the first hour** of the outward and homeward journey. Always at BHR. |
| **Asked to collect equipment / personnel** | If asked to collect from any address other than your home, you're paid for that time as **working time, base to base**. |

### 3.2 — Travel Expenses

**50p / mile** for car use, except:
- **Studio shoot** — expenses don't apply.
- **Within M25** — no payment for the first leg. If multiple locations within M25, mileage is paid from first to second and all subsequent moves.
- **Outside M25** — 50p/mile calculated from W1F 9SE to location and back, per the Movement Order from Location Manager / Production.

For non-London prod cos, use the same formula with a **20-mile radius from the production company's address**.

| Location example | Mileage |
|---|---|
| Within M25 | no payment |
| Windsor Castle (SL4 1NJ ≈ 23 mi) | 2 × 23 × £0.50 = **£23** |

### 3.3 — Travel by Air

- Air travel = scheduled passenger service. Meals & refreshments provided on flights and stopovers.
- **Flight time > 4 hours** → no shooting on the day of the flight (except in exceptional circumstances).
- **Scheduled flight time > 8 hours** → no shooting until **24 hours after arrival** (except in exceptional circumstances).

## 4 — Overtime

OT formula: **BHR × OT coefficient** (per grade). §4 does not apply to PMs, PAs or Runners.

### 4.1 — Grade I — BDR £0–£444 → **1.5 × BHR**

*Example:* Carpenter — BDR £386, BHR £39, OT £58, Triple £116.

### 4.2 — Grade II — BDR £445–£676 → **1.25 × BHR**

*Example:* Location Manager — BDR £580, BHR £58, OT £73, Triple £174.

### 4.3 — Grade III — BDR £677+ → **1.0 × BHR**

*Example:* DoP — BDR £1,516, BHR £152, OT £152, Triple £455.

### 4.4 — Overtime After Midnight

**3 × BHR (triple time)** for all OT worked **00:00–05:00** and continuously thereafter until wrap.

### 4.5 — OT Charge Rounding

OT is charged **per minute** but **rounded up to 30 mins** in the crew's favour. (10 OT mins → 30 mins of OT charged.)

### 4.6 — OT Rates on Saturdays

**1.5 × BHR** for all hours worked, **min 10h call**. OT after midnight is still **3 × BHR**. Going past 05:00 = continuous triple time until wrap (not a new day).

### 4.7 — OT Rates on Sundays / Bank Holidays / Statutory Holidays

**2 × BHR** for all hours worked, **min 10h call**. OT after midnight is still **3 × BHR**. Past 05:00 = continuous triple time until wrap.

>
> | Crew | BDR | BHR | 2 × BHR |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Carpenter | £386 | £39 | £77 |
> | Location Manager | £580 | £58 | £116 |

## 5 — Time Off The Clock (TOC)

If production lasts more than 1 day, the **minimum break between wrap and the next day's call is 11 hours** ("time off the clock"). The hard floor is **10 hours** — crew may be engaged for up to **1 hour of TOC** in respect of any one break.

**If TOC is reduced to 10 hours**, the one TOC hour worked is paid at the **standard OT rate** *in addition to* being paid for those hours as part of the basic day rate.

§5 does not apply to PMs, PAs or Runners.

*Example — Construction Manager (BDR £532, BHR £53, OT £67, TOC penalty £67):*

| | Charge breakdown | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 (08:00–23:00) | 1 × £532 (BDR) + 4 × £67 (OT) | **£800** |
| Day 2 (09:00–20:00, only 10h TOC) | 1 × £67 (TOC) + 1 × £532 (BDR) + 1 × £67 (OT) | **£666** |

## 6 — Breaks and Penalties

§6 does not apply to PMs, PAs or Runners. Two breaks on each shooting day. Same provisions every day, including weekends and bank/stat holidays.

### 6.1 — Breakfast

Provided as a courtesy — **not compulsory**. No compensation if not provided.

### 6.2 — First Break (1 hour, **unpaid** — not part of the working day)

Begins **no more than 5.5 hours after work has commenced**. Lunch is provided on **Shoot Days and Pre-light Days only** (see §2.3).

| Scenario | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Delayed** | £10 penalty. **If 1st break does not commence within 6.5 hours of main unit call, the day becomes a CWD** (no late-lunch penalty applies). |
| **Curtailed** | OT commences 11h from call **less the curtailment** (e.g. curtailed by 20 min → OT starts at 10h 40m). If no OT worked, crew is paid for the curtailed minutes at single time. |
| **Missed** | If 6.5h have elapsed since main unit call, the day is treated as a **CWD**. |
| **Compensation** | Free meal **or** £7.50 meal allowance. |
| **Missed on a night shoot** | Charged at BHR. |

### 6.3 — Second Break (30 min, **paid** — part of the working day)

Begins **no more than 5.5 hours after the end of the first break**.

| Scenario | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Delayed** | Cannot be delayed — the break is either given or missed. |
| **Curtailed** | Paid for the curtailed minutes. |
| **Missed** | 30 min × BHR penalty. |
| **Compensation** | None financial if food not provided. |
| **Missed on a night shoot** | Charged at BHR. |

### 6.4 — Additional Break on Continuous Working Day (CWD only)

A 30-minute break beginning **no more than 12.5h from call**, **preceded** by a 30-minute break (per §6.3 second-break provisions) **after 9h from call**. Both **paid** (part of the working day).

| Scenario | Rule |
|---|---|
| Length | 30 minutes |
| Delayed | Cannot be delayed — given by 12.5h or missed (penalty). |
| Missed | 30 min × BHR. |
| Compensation | N/A. |

## 7 — Cancellation Fees

If production is cancelled for any reason **except Force Majeure**, the cancellation fee is calculated as below.

**Notice period:** all 7 days of the week count. The day notice is given **is** included; the shoot day **is not**.

**Engagement length:**
- ≤ 3 days → cancellation fee applies to the whole engagement.
- > 3 days → each day is a separate engagement, fee calculated per day. **Build, recce, pre-light, shoot, and strike days all count as engagements.**

| Notice given | Fee |
|---|---|
| 7+ days before | **0%** (no fee) |
| 6 – 4 days before | **50%** of agreed fee |
| 3 – 2 days before | **75%** of agreed fee |
| Day before engagement | **100%** of agreed fee |

**Long-engagement crew** (e.g. Art Directors, Production Managers, Location Managers): if work has commenced before cancellation, they're paid for work already done plus **reasonable compensation** having regard to their obligation to seek replacement work.

#### Example — cancelling a 2-day shoot (Sat shoot 1, Sun shoot 2)

Notice given on... | Sat shoot fee | Sun shoot fee
---|---|---
Friday (1 wk before) | 0% | 0%
Saturday | 50% | 50%
Sunday | 50% | 50%
Monday | 50% | 75%
Tuesday | 75% | 75%
Wednesday | 75% | 100%
Thursday | 100% | (shoot day)

(Above is illustrative — apply the table per day for any pattern.)

#### Example — cancelling a 4-day shoot (Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun)

Use the same per-day calculation (>3 days = each day separate). Days that are 7+ days out at notice = 0%; 6–4 days out = 50%; 3–2 days out = 75%; day before = 100%.

## 8 — Insurance

The production company arranges insurance for crew working **overseas** or on a **hazardous UK location**. Terms provided in writing on request.

---

## 9 — Assignment of Services

The production company may assign the benefit of your services under this agreement, but **remains obliged to pay** you everything you're entitled to.

---

## 10 — Holiday Pay

Each crew member is entitled to **5.6 weeks' paid holiday** per holiday year (incl. all bank-holiday entitlements), pro-rated by hours actually worked. The entitlement is **12.07% of hours worked**, rounded up to the nearest hour. **Crew payments include a payment in lieu of the 12.07% holiday entitlement** (i.e. holiday pay is rolled in unless agreed otherwise).

---

## 11 — Force Majeure

If your engagement is cancelled because of an event of Force Majeure (as defined in **Appendix 2**), the production company pays you for work done up to the point production ceased — and is not liable for any other payments.

#### (c) Programmable Lighting Desk Operator role definition ***

An electrician operating and programming a lighting desk requiring lighting cues during a take, effects (flicker / fire), control of moving lights or multi-channel LEDs, or time-coded / midi / analogue synchronisation and triggering (motion control). The rate / grade applies **only when** the position is deemed necessary and **agreed in advance between the Gaffer and Production Manager**. Does **not** apply to a basic fader lighting desk operated by an electrician.

## Appendix 2 — Force Majeure Definition

A Force Majeure event is any event that is **not reasonably insurable**, including (but not limited to):

- Any act of terrorism or threat of terrorism.
- Any hostile or war-like action in time of peace or war.
- The use or threat of use of any weapon employing atomic fission or radioactive force.
- Any insurrection, rebellion, revolution, civil war, or usurped power.
- Any action by a governmental authority in hindering, combating, or defending against such occurrence.
- Seizure or destruction under quarantine or customs regulation, or confiscation by order of any government or public authority.
- Risks of contraband or illegal transportation of trade.
- Any civil commotion amounting to a popular rising, riot, or martial law.
- Any act of any lawfully constituted civil authority — except to the extent that certain acts of civil authority may reasonably be insurable from time to time.
