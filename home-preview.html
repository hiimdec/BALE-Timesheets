Built for actual commercials

Different days, not one timer.

A TVC week is not a stopwatch with overtime bolted on. The days have different shapes, and the pay follows the shape. The app knows which day it is.

SHOOT PRE-LIGHT RECCE PREP TRAVEL DERIG OT L1 L2 CWD TOC NIGHT EARLY CALL MILEAGE PER DIEM

A travel day pays your basic hourly rate, on a minimum, not a flat day rate.

A pre-light day is eight hours plus a lunch hour, not the eleven of a shoot day.

A continuous working day starts overtime after nine, not eleven, and you can land on one without being told.

A night shoot is flat double hourly with a ten hour minimum, no overtime on top.

Six fields, and not the rest.

The honest version is the strong one. It takes what helps you get paid and leaves what would only mislead.

  • It reads the invoicing details. The fields that are the same every day of the job and tedious to retype.
  • It does not take call times. Calls are often individual and they change, so importing them would import something likely to be wrong. Entering your own call takes a second.
  • It does not take wrap. Wrap on a call sheet is an estimate. The app assumes a full day with no overtime, and you set wrap when the day actually runs long. Wrapping early costs you nothing.
  • Nothing else on a call sheet is any use for getting paid.
iPhone only. It needs iOS 26 on an Apple Intelligence capable device, and it runs entirely on your phone: the call sheet is never uploaded. The web app does not have this.
