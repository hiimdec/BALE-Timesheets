# Pact/Bectu published rate cards — transcribed source

These are the published rate cards, transcribed from the figures the founder
supplied (Phase 4g). They are SOURCE documents, kept for provenance and for
validating the engine's divisors against numbers produced independently of the
agreements themselves. Where a card and an agreement disagree on a rate, the
card is the money the crew is actually paid; the agreement remains the source
of the RULES (day shapes, triggers, uplifts). None of these numbers is wired
into the calc engine as an autofill — the user enters their own rate (which
may differ from the card by deal). See CALC_DECISIONS.md / PACT_BECTU_PLAN.md.

If the founder can supply the original PDFs, add them alongside this file and
name each by its CONTENT (as the plan file requires), not its download name.

## Pact/Bectu Lighting Technicians' Pay Increase — 20 December 2024 (Scripted TV)

Rates per standard working day, by budget band, for 2025 and 2026:

| Band | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Band 1 | £324.70 | £332.81 |
| Band 2 | £334.07 | £342.42 |
| Band 3 | £349.67 | £358.42 |
| Band 4 | £368.11 | £377.31 |

Notes carried on the card:

- **"All rates apply to a standard 10 hour working day (i.e. 10+1 SWD / 9 CWD /
  9.5 SCWD)."** This independently confirms the ruleset's TV shooting hours: a
  10-hour SWD (10 worked + 1 lunch), a 9-hour CWD, a 9.5-hour SCWD.
- **Rates are inclusive of holiday pay.** This is why the app scoping holiday
  pay out is safe — the entered daily rate already contains it. The app never
  models holiday pay, gross-only (see the plan file's PAYE ruling).
- **Chargehands: the electricians' rate plus 20%, on Band 4 only.** No uplift on
  Bands 1 to 3. A role-based rate rule the rulebook does not currently carry.

## Pact/Bectu MMP rate card — 1 April 2026 (Film)

| Role | Shooting | Rigging |
|---|---|---|
| Lighting technicians | £441.92 (11hr + 1hr lunch SWD, or 10hr CWD) | £361.58 (9hr + 1hr lunch SWD) |
| Chargehands rigging | — | £441.09 |
| Rigging supervisor | — | £490.70 |
| Console operators | £582.03 | £496.84 |
| Practical electricians | same as lighting technicians | — |

**The film card validates the engine's divisors, independently of the
agreement.** The same hourly rate is reached two ways:

- Shooting: £441.92 ÷ 11 = **£40.17/hr**
- Rigging:  £361.58 ÷  9 = **£40.18/hr**

A shooting technician's 11-hour day and a rigging technician's 9-hour day pay
the same per hour — confirming the ruleset's film divisors (÷11 standard, ÷9
rigging) from a document produced separately from the MMP agreement. Pinned
(LF21).
