Nine hours, no breaks, overtime from nine hours not eleven. If your first break starts late enough, an ordinary day converts automatically, and nobody announces it.

Night shoots: what you're actually owed

Flat double hourly with a ten hour minimum, no overtime, no triple. Unless it's a continuous night, which pays more and looks identical on the call sheet.
