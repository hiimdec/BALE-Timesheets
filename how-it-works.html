Questions you might have

FAQ.

Does it cover my role?

+

If you work on commercials under APA terms, yes — Sparks, Grips, Construction, Art Dept, Camera, PMs, PAs, Runners. The app applies the right §s of the rate card to each role (e.g. §5 TOC and §6 breaks don't apply to PMs/PAs/Runners — the app knows). Custom BDR for anything bespoke.

Where can I run it?

+

Any modern browser, right now. iPhone, Android, laptop, all of it. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen and it behaves like a native app. A proper iOS App Store version is in the pipeline.

Does it work offline?

+

Right now it's a web app, so it needs an internet connection to load. Your data lives locally once you're in, but you'll need signal to open it fresh. Proper offline support is on the list for the iOS version — useful when you're rigging in a barn with two bars.

What if production disputes my total?

+

Every PDF and text export shows the full breakdown — base day rate, every penalty applied, every multiplier, every extra. If they want to argue a number, they can argue the APA terms. The PDF also shows which rate version was used (currently APA Sept 2025).

Will the rates stay up to date?

+

When the APA publishes a new rate card, the app updates and the new version shows in Settings → About. Old productions keep the rates they were costed under.

What's coming next?

+

iOS native app via TestFlight, then App Store. Proper offline mode. More invoice templates. Anything you ask for that more than three people also want.
