The details that matter

The little stuff most apps miss.

Pre-call at BHR

Truck drivers, pre-light shifts, anyone with an early start - pre-call hours paid at base hourly, lunch deadline left intact.

Step-up rates

Asked to cover the Gaffer for the day? Flag the step-up - the higher rate applies for that day, automatically.

Auto CWD detection

Turnaround drops below APA minimums and the app flags a Continuous Working Day before you do, with the right rate.

Favourable rounding

Where the APA terms allow benefit-of-the-doubt rounding, you get it. Toggle off if you prefer the strict reading.

Questions you might have

FAQ.

Does it cover my role? +

If you work on commercials under APA terms, yes - Sparks, Grips, Construction, Art Dept, Camera, PMs, PAs, Runners. The app applies the right §s of the rate card to each role (e.g. §5 TOC and §6 breaks don't apply to PMs/PAs/Runners - the app knows). Custom BDR for anything bespoke.

Where can I run it? +

Any modern browser, right now - iPhone, Android, laptop. On iPhone there's a native app on the App Store. On Android or desktop, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and the web app behaves like an installed one.

Does it work offline? +

The web app needs a connection to load - it's a website, so there's nothing to download. The iPhone app is different: it installs on the phone, so it opens and runs without signal, handy when you're rigging somewhere with two bars. Your data is local either way.

What if production disputes my total? +

Every PDF and text export shows the full breakdown - base day rate, every penalty applied, every multiplier, every extra. If they want to argue a number, they can argue the APA terms. The PDF also shows which rate version was used (currently APA Sept 2025).

Will the rates stay up to date? +

When the APA publishes a new rate card, the app updates and the new version shows in Settings → About. Old productions keep the rates they were costed under.
